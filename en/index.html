BoardStamp JOB SITE PHOTOS Get the app

Android app · No account needed

No whiteboard needed.
The site record is on the photo.

BoardStamp is a timestamp camera for job site and construction photos. It puts your project, work type, location, description and date on each photo as a clean table stamp and saves it straight to your album.

iPhone coming soon

  • Photos stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Shooting and stamping work without internet.
  • No sign-up or login.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, done

1

Fill it in once

Enter the project, work type, location and description. They stay for your next photos.

2

Tap Shoot

Take the photo with your phone camera. Date and address are filled in automatically.

3

Saved to your album

The stamped photo goes into the album for that site, ready for your daily photo report.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free? Are there ads?

It's free to use. There is one small banner ad below the photo on the result screen.

Does it work without internet?

Shooting and stamping work offline. Filling in the address automatically may need an internet connection.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos and stamp details stay on your phone, and there's no account.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's on the way. For now, BoardStamp is available on Android.

Can I stamp photos I already took?

Yes. Tap Import, pick several photos from your album and stamp them all at once.
